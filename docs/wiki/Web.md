# Stați on the web

**Updated on** November 11, 2026 · **Updated by** Codex for Stați

Open the [Stați website](https://stati.ciani01.org) for the web experience. Invite links and receiver links may open a specific screen. Your account and computer approval determine what you can access.

## Sign in and submit

1. Open the link from your church computer or administrator.
2. Sign in with the passkey tied to your Stați account if prompted.
3. Confirm the receiving computer and select your material.
4. Wait for the upload status before closing the tab. If your network drops, check the result before submitting a second copy.

For a church presentation computer, use the [Windows app](https://github.com/Hapi-Products/stati/wiki/Windows) or a supported [Mac preview](https://github.com/Hapi-Products/stati/wiki/macOS).
