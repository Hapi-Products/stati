# Stați Worship for iPad

**Updated on** November 11, 2026 · **Updated by** Codex for Stați

Stați Worship gives iPad users **Recents**, **Groups**, **Songs**, and **Service**. The computer you select and your church role determine the material you can open.

## Read songs during a service

1. Open **Service** and choose the plan for the service.
2. Tap a song to open it in the reader. Use the page controls to move through the song.
3. Open the page counter to pick from slide previews. Use the song selector to jump to another song.
4. Return to **Service** to see the running order again. Use **Songs** when you need to search by title or lyrics.

**Groups** gives you access to shared files. Use the people icon or pick a worship group in the sidebar. The group opens within Groups. Leaving the tab clears your temporary selection. To change the default, use **Account → Worship**. **Recents** is a quick way back to a song or file you already opened. Your account can have saved offline song copies from the connected station.

## Refresh or lead a service

Open a service to see its running order in the Service tab. Use the refresh icon in the navigation bar for the latest order. The person icon appears when your role allows you to lead, take over, or leave leadership. Use the back arrow to return to the service list. Switching tabs closes the current service detail.

## Change the iPad settings

Open your account, then **Features** for reader, library, and update controls or **Worship** for group choices. Apple’s Settings organizes these preferences under **Settings → Apps → Stați → Features**. The system page lists fixed choices for the default group. Pick a particular named group or computer inside Stați.
