# Worship settings on iPad

**Updated on** November 11, 2026 · **Updated by** Codex for Stați

These steps describe development build 41. You can edit Worship preferences in **Stați → Account → Features** and under **Settings → Apps → Stați**. The two screens use the same iPad preferences. Reopen Stați after making a change in Apple’s Settings.

Development build 42 adds a lightbulb button in Features. Tap it for the native guide, then choose **Step-by-step guide** to work through the instructions one at a time.

## Make songs easier to read

1. Open **Open songs as** and choose Slides, PDF document, or Text.
2. For Slides, set **Slide text size**. Open one of your longer songs to check readability.
3. For PDF, set **PDF columns** and **PDF page effect**. Use one column when you need the wider page.
4. Adjust **Song previews** for larger or standard thumbnails. Use **Song library layout** to choose Grid or List.

## Choose what the reader follows

- **Song selector** can show Recents or the active service. If there is no active service, the selector falls back to Recents.
- **Clicker** controls support for an external page-turning clicker.
- **Remove from Recents** controls how long the viewing history stays. It applies to history entries.
- **Default group** offers Automatic, Original songs, All groups, or the group selected in Stați. Use **Account → Worship** for a specific named group or folder.
- **Default computer** is available inside Stați, where the picker can show your connected computers.

## Appearance, updates, and the Lock Screen

Choose an **App icon** in Settings and return to Stați for it to apply. The in-app picker lets you preview the choices. Turn **Show What’s New after updates** on or off according to your preference. **Live song on Lock Screen** is an opt-in preview. A working church setup also needs the compatible presentation computer and server feed.
