# Worship settings on iPad

**Updated on** November 11, 2026 · **Updated by** Codex for Stați

Open **Account → Features** inside the app to edit Worship preferences. Saved reader choices carry forward. In Apple Settings, the app’s page shows the version and where to find Diagnostics. The development build is called **Stazi Beta** and uses a fixed blue icon and accent. The regular build offers the app icon picker. In Stazi Beta build 50, **Developer Mode** in Features opts this device into Stați shortcuts on iPadOS 27.3 or later.

The Songs controls use two columns when there’s room. Tap the lightbulb beside Songs to start a guide. The pop-up dims the surrounding screen and highlights the control it describes. The arrow buttons move through the guide, and the book icon opens this wiki page. Use the gear at the top of Features to open Apple Settings.

## Make songs easier to read

1. Open **Open songs as** and choose Slides, PDF document, or Text.
2. For Slides, tap a point on the **Slide text size** track or drag the thumb. The size changes continuously and saves when you let go. Open a longer song to check readability.
3. Open **PDF Layout** to set columns and the page transition. These settings apply when you open a song as a PDF. Choose one column for a wider page.
4. Turn **Larger previews** on for bigger thumbnails. Use **Song library layout** to choose Grid or List.

## Choose what the reader follows

- **Song selector** can show Recents or the active service. If there is no active service, the selector falls back to Recents.
- **Clicker** controls support for an external page-turning clicker.
- **Remove from Recents** controls how long the viewing history stays. It applies to history entries.
- **Default group** offers Automatic, Original songs, All groups, or the group selected in Stați. Use **Account → Worship** for a specific named group or folder.
- **Default computer** is available inside Stați, where the picker can show your connected computers.

## Appearance, Siri, updates, and the Lock Screen

In the regular build, choose an **App icon** in Features. The picker lets you preview the choices. Turn **Show What’s New after updates** on or off according to your preference. **Live song on Lock Screen** is an opt-in preview. A working church setup also needs the compatible presentation computer and server feed.

Turn on Siri Shortcuts or Siri AI Song Search in **Features → Siri**. See [Siri and Shortcuts](https://github.com/Hapi-Products/stati/wiki/Siri-and-Shortcuts) for phrases, requirements, and how to run an action by hand.
