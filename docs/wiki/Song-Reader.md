# Song reader on iPad

**Updated on** November 12, 2026 · **Updated by** Codex for Stați

## Read a song

1. Open **Browse** and select a result, or open **Service** and choose the song in the running order.
2. Use the arrows in the glass page control to move one page at a time. The page counter in the middle opens thumbnails. When there are multiple pages, you can also tap the left or right edge of the reader.
3. Open the page previews from the page counter. Select a thumbnail to jump to that slide.
4. Open the song selector to jump to a different song. The selector can show Recents or the active service.

## Set up the reader

In **Account → Features**, choose the reading format, size, and song selector. Open **PDF Layout** to choose the number of columns and page transition for the PDF reader.

Tap the question mark in the song reader toolbar. The first pop-up highlights Song Selector. For songs with multiple slides, the next pop-up points to the page counter. The book icon opens this page. Use the focus button in the lower right to hide or restore the reader controls. Text uses a larger reading size. The PDF reader follows the app’s light or dark appearance.
