# Song reader on iPad

**Updated on** November 11, 2026 · **Updated by** Codex for Stați

## Read a song

1. Open **Songs** and select a result, or open **Service** and choose the song in the running order.
2. Use the arrows in the page controls to move one page at a time. When there are multiple pages, you can also tap the left or right edge of the reader.
3. Open the page previews from the page counter. Select a thumbnail to jump to that slide.
4. Open the song selector to jump to a different song. The selector can show Recents or the active service.

## Set up the reader

In **Account → Features**, choose the reading format, size, and song selector. Open **PDF Layout** to choose the number of columns and page transition for the PDF reader.

In development build 45, tap the question mark in the song reader toolbar. The first pop-up highlights Song Selector. For songs with multiple slides, the next pop-up points to the page counter. The book icon opens this page.
