# Song reader on iPad

**Updated on** November 11, 2026 · **Updated by** Codex for Stați

## Read a song

1. Open **Songs** and select a result, or open **Service** and choose the song in the running order.
2. In the slides view, use the left or right side of the reader to move one slide at a time.
3. Open the page previews from the page counter. Select a thumbnail to jump to that slide.
4. Open the song selector to jump to a different song. The selector can show Recents or the active service.

## Set up the reader

In **Account → Features**, choose the reading format, size, and song selector. On iPad, open **Settings → Apps → Stați → Features** for these preferences in development build 43.

In development build 45, tap the question mark in the song reader toolbar. The first pop-up points to Song Selector. For songs with multiple slides, the next pop-up points to the page counter. The book icon opens this page.
