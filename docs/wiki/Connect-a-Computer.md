# Connect a computer

**Updated on** November 11, 2026 · **Updated by** Codex for Stați

## Pair or request access

1. Ask the church operator to open Stați on the computer you need.
2. Choose the relevant action on the computer. Use the pairing or access QR for connecting an account and the receiver QR when submitting a file.
3. On your phone, scan the code using the app’s camera action when available or your device camera.
4. Confirm the computer name. Sign in to your existing account if prompted.
5. If the request needs approval, ask the operator to review it. Wait for the computer to appear in your list.

## Pick your usual computer

In the app, open your profile or account and select the default computer. You can switch destinations before sending. A computer may be connected to your account yet offline at the time you try to use it.

If a code expires, generate a new one at the source. If the computer is missing after approval, refresh the list with the same account that requested access.
