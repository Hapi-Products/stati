# Stați for iPhone

**Updated on** November 13, 2026 · **Updated by** Codex for Stați

The iPhone app combines **Send**, **Tasks**, **Profile**, and song search where your role allows it. The current development builds target iOS 26.3 or later. Check [Updates](https://github.com/Hapi-Products/stati/wiki/Updates) for the status of a specific build.

Find the current build’s highlights and earlier updates in **Profile → What’s New**.

The development build appears as **Stazi Beta** with a fixed blue icon and accent. The regular app offers the icon picker in Profile.

## Send a file or song text

1. Open **Send** and select the intended computer under the heading.
2. Tap the add button. Choose a file, song file, photo, or **Song text**.
3. If you chose **Song text**, enter a title and lyrics, then tap the checkmark.
4. Review the items in the queue and tap **Send**. Check the status for each item.
5. For eligible song submissions, **Save for Today** gives you a copy for the day while the computer reviews the submission.

## Pause builds during service

In development build 63, administrators can open **Profile → Features** and turn on **Pause Builds**. Turn it off to clear the manual pause. The toggle updates the release hold for Stați, including preview installs.

Scheduled services, a service selected on the computer, and active live readers also hold releases automatically. If a finished service is still selected, choose **None · Lyrics only** on the computer. A held build can be retried once all holds clear.

In builds 57–62, use the pause icon at the top of **Send**.

## Use iPhone as a live clicker

This feature requires build 58 on the iPhone and the leader’s iPad and the updated Stați server.

1. On iPad, start leading the active service for your worship group and open a song as **Slides** or a **PDF**.
2. On iPhone, sign in with the same leader profile and select the same computer in **Send**.
3. Tap the clicker icon at the top of **Send**. The screen shows the live song and page. Use the arrows to turn pages on the leader’s iPad and the tablets following it.
4. Tap **×** to leave the clicker. The service continues. If iOS Live Activities are enabled, tap the activity on the Lock Screen or Dynamic Island to reopen the clicker. You can also reopen it from **Send**.

In build 59, the clicker previews the live slide or PDF page. The layout icon in the top bar lets you choose **Middle** or **Bottom** for the arrows. Stați remembers your choice. Use the refresh icon in the top bar to retry the connection.

The iPad leader can also open a song from **Browse** and tap **Lead song** without an active service. Connect from the same iPhone clicker as usual.

The iPad needs to stay in the live reader with an internet connection. If leadership changes or the service ends, the phone can no longer turn its pages.

With build 60 and the updated server, Stați tells connected devices as soon as the live page changes. If that connection drops, the clicker and reader keep checking for the current page.

In development build 62, the clicker uses the confirmed page from each tap so you can press the next arrow as soon as Stați accepts the previous turn.

With development build 63 and the accompanying server update, keeping the clicker open lets the leader’s iPad show **Phone clicker connected** in the Service ready check.

### Test the clicker before service

Open **Service → Ready check → Clicker Test** on the iPad. On iPhone, open **Live Clicker** from Send, tap the options icon, and choose **Clicker Test**. Use the same profile and computer on both devices. Start the test and leave both screens open for ten seconds. Stați sends five sample pages and shows how many the iPad acknowledged, plus the median time from the server receiving a page to the iPad’s acknowledgment. The test runs separately from the live reader.

In build 64, swipe the live preview to turn pages, or tap the page count to choose a specific page. The preview uses the PDF transition from the leader’s iPad and respects Reduce Motion.

## Find songs and use Shortcuts

Use the search icon and enter a title or part of a lyric. Choose the matching version from the results. The **Find a Song** Siri shortcut opens matching library results for you to choose from. Turn on Siri Shortcuts in **Profile → Features**.

In Stazi Beta, enable **Developer Mode** in **Profile → Features** to run Stați shortcuts on iOS 27.3 or later. On a compatible device, turn on **Siri AI Song Search** there too. See [Siri and Shortcuts](https://github.com/Hapi-Products/stati/wiki/Siri-and-Shortcuts) for available actions and example phrases. Open **Profile → Diagnostics** to see whether the device meets the Siri AI preview requirements, review recent errors or report a problem privately. **Save Song to a Service** opens a service picker for accounts that can plan services.

Song comments and corrections depend on your role and the song you opened. For an authorized correction, open the song and choose the correction action. Review the selected text before submitting.
