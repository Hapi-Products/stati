# Siri and Shortcuts on iPhone and iPad

**Updated on** November 12, 2026 · **Updated by** Codex for Stați

## Run a shortcut

1. Sign in to Stați. On iPhone, open **Profile → Features**. On iPad, open **Account → Features**.
2. In **Stazi Beta** build 50 or later, turn on **Developer Mode** in Features. The switch is available on iOS and iPadOS 27.3 or later. In the regular app, turn on **Siri Shortcuts**.
3. Open **Shortcuts** and look for Stați to run its actions directly.
4. Try the phrases below. If Siri hears the name differently, run the action in Shortcuts or rename a personal shortcut to a phrase that Siri recognizes.

| Try saying | Stați action |
| --- | --- |
| “Find a song in Stați” | Prompts for the title or part of a lyric and opens library results. |
| “Open a song in Stați” | Runs the same search. Select the version yourself from the results. |
| “Browse songs in Stați” or “Show all songs in Stați” | Opens the alphabetical song library. |
| “Show services in Stați” | Opens Services. |
| “Show recent songs in Stați” | Opens Recents. |
| “Save this song to a service in Stați” | Opens the service picker for the song on screen. |
| “Add a song to a service in Stați” | Opens song search when no song is open. |

Use the app name shown in Shortcuts when speaking a phrase. The phrases above are registered in English. You can type a Romanian title or lyric in the shortcut or in Stați’s song search if Siri mishears it. A search returns songs available to your signed-in account.

## Place a song in the running order

1. Open the song and say “Save this song to a service in Stazi Beta” in the development build. You can also use **Add to service** in the song menu.
2. Choose an active service on the selected computer. You need permission to edit its running order.
3. Drag the new song by its handle. Tap **Done** to save the order to the computer’s shared service plan. The station uses its own playlist review and sync settings.

## Try Siri AI search

On a compatible device, enable Siri AI in Apple Settings and turn on **Siri AI Song Search** in Stați’s Features. Try “Search Stați for Amazing Grace.” This is a preview of Apple’s in-app search action. It searches the Stați library and lets you choose a result. Siri recognition and whether it routes a spoken request to Stați depend on Apple’s supported devices, languages, and settings. In Stazi Beta, Developer Mode enables the regular shortcuts even if the device does not support Apple Intelligence. Siri AI Song Search also requires Apple Intelligence. The developer preview requires iOS or iPadOS 27.3 or later and Apple Intelligence. In **Diagnostics**, the Siri AI preview entry shows when the OS is older, the device is unsupported, Apple Intelligence is off, or its models are preparing. The same status appears in copied diagnostics.
