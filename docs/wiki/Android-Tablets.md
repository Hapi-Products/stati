# Stați for Android tablets

**Updated on** November 11, 2026 · **Updated by** Codex for Stați

Use the [Stați website](https://stati.ciani01.org) or the Android package provided by your church. The current Android source opens the same web experience on phones and tablets. Separate Android tablet editions for Worship and Pastor are planned.

## Use the tablet at church

1. Open Stați and sign in with your usual account.
2. Open the section available to your role. For file submission, scan the church computer’s receiver QR.
3. Keep the receipt or status visible until you can confirm the computer received the material.
4. If the browser interrupts sign-in or file selection, reopen the site in the browser that holds your passkey and check the action again.

The iPad Worship reader and its Apple Settings controls are specific to [Stați Worship for iPad](https://github.com/Hapi-Products/stati/wiki/iPadOS).
